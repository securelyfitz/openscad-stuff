#GPD Win Max 2 under-desk mounting tray

This is a tray designed to screw into the bottom of a desk so that you can slide the GPD Win Max 2 into it and have it line up with a USB connector at the back.
There is also a tray designed to hold the amazon basics thunderbolt 4 dock in place. For some reason, this is no longer available, but the scad might be useful for other docks.

1. place the win max 2 in place
1. connect the USB cable (or thunderbolt cable, etc)
1. screw an M3 bolt into the hole to lock the USB cable in place
1. slide the win max 2 in and out to make sure it lines up.

If the usb port is too high, a few layers of tape should help line it up.
